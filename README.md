## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **We set up your AI receptionist** – trained on your business: services, hours, FAQs, pricing
2. **Connect your calendar** – Google Calendar sync for real-time availability
3. **Calls get answered 24/7** – the AI greets callers, answers questions, books appointments
4. **You get summaries** – every call's transcript, recording, and booking details
5. **Missed-call text-back** – if a call can't connect, an SMS goes out instantly

**Setup time:** ~10 minutes. **Free 14-day pilot** – no credit card required.
